========================================
WHY THESE CHANGES (context for the model)
========================================
Research across competitor reviews and studies shows: ~70% of health-app users churn within 100 days; the top abandonment causes are entry effort, guilt (broken streaks, red missed days, scolding reminders), data without meaning, and paywalling core features. The most-loved apps win with ≤10-second typing-free entry, shame-free tone, plain-language insight, and a "showed my doctor" payoff. Our positioning: finite recovery with a finish line — the defining user question is "Am I on track?"

========================================
CHANGE 1 — CHECK-IN: ADD A ≤10-SECOND FAST PATH (highest priority)
========================================
Restructure the Daily Check-in screen into two layers on one screen:
- TOP (dominant, thumb zone): "How's today?" with three large buttons — Better / Same / Worse (icon + word, never color-only). ONE tap = a complete, valid check-in → gentle confirmation (checkmark draw-on ~600ms + light haptic) → done. No scrolling needed.
- BELOW (optional, clearly secondary): "Add detail" — the existing mood/pain slider/energy/sleep/mobility/medication/note controls, collapsed by default.
Annotate: exact button sizes (≥44pt, likely much larger), pressed states, the confirmation choreography, and how the detail layer expands (duration/easing).

========================================
CHANGE 2 — HOME: ADD THE QUICK CAPTURE FIELD (the "Notes-app" lane)
========================================
Add a persistent single-line capture field on Home, high in the layout: placeholder "Note anything… ('knee hurt after stairs')", with a microphone affordance inviting dictation, and one Save action. Zero other required inputs — no category, no tags, no questions. Saving shows a subtle "Added to your timeline" toast.
Also design (as a separate frame) a HOME-SCREEN WIDGET mockup: compact widget with Day N + one-tap "Capture" and "Check in" shortcuts (for a later native phase — design it now).
Annotate: field autofocus behavior, keyboard handling, save micro-interaction, toast timing.

========================================
CHANGE 3 — REMOVE ALL GUILT MECHANICS (structural, app-wide)
========================================
- Replace any streak/chain visual with gentle ACCUMULATION: "12 check-ins" counter or softly filled dots — nothing that can visually "break."
- Timeline gaps: empty days render as a soft neutral "rest" treatment (muted, calm), NEVER red, alert-colored, or labeled as missed.
- Design a "Welcome back" state for returning after several days: warm one-liner, no recap of missed days, straight into the fast-path check-in.
- Reminder/notification design mock: friendly, single, non-escalating tone example.
Annotate the gap treatment (exact colors/opacity) and welcome-back entry animation.
