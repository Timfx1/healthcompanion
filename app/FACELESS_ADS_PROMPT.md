## THE APP — AnklePath (full context)

**What it is:** AnklePath is a guided ankle-injury recovery companion app for iOS and Android. It turns "I hurt my ankle and don't know what to do" into a clear, personalized, day-by-day recovery plan you follow at home.

**Who it's for (target audience):**
- People who just rolled/sprained an ankle and are anxious about what to do next.
- Recurring/chronic ankle instability — ankles that keep "giving way."
- Weekend warriors and amateur athletes who want to return to sport without re-injuring.
- Post-surgery / post-op ankle recovery, unsure what movement is safe.
- Sports ankle pain and Grade 1/2/3 sprains.
- People who can't quickly or affordably get physio (cost, long waitlists) and want structured, trustworthy guidance at home.
- People currently doing random YouTube exercises and unsure if they're helping or hurting.

**How it works:**
- **Personalized onboarding** asks: injury type (recent sprain, severe/Grade 3, chronic instability, post-surgery, sports pain, "not sure yet"), when it happened, symptoms (swelling, bruising, pain when walking, can't bear weight, stiffness, weakness, instability, limited motion), pain level (0–10), walking ability, recovery goal (walk without pain, return to sport, reduce swelling, regain mobility, rebuild strength, prevent re-injury), and reminders.
- **Builds a phased recovery plan** that progresses through stages — Phase 1: Protect & Reduce Swelling → range of motion → strength → balance → return to sport. It shows a live **"Day N since injury"** counter so recovery feels tracked and finite.
- **Daily home dashboard** with:
  - **Today's exercises** — short, video-guided routines (e.g., ankle circles, towel stretch, resistance-band eversion, single-leg balance) with sets/reps, difficulty, step-by-step cues, and safety caveats. Exercise videos are sourced from real physio providers.
  - **Pain check-in** — log pain score, symptoms, location, and notes each day.
  - **Trackers** — swelling, walking, range of motion, and balance, charted over time.
  - **Learn hub** — short, plain-English, evidence-based articles sourced from NHS, Mayo Clinic, Cleveland Clinic, OrthoInfo (AAOS), and ChoosePT (APTA). Topics include: the first 48 hours (R.I.C.E.), sprain grades, ice vs heat, how long swelling lasts, when you can walk again, braces/supports, progressing exercises safely, return to sport, high ankle sprains, and warning signs.
  - **Safety / red flags** — clear "when to see a doctor" guidance.
- **Recovery reports** — an exportable summary of your pain logs, symptoms, and tracker trends that you can **show your physio or doctor** (for your own tracking and communication — not a medical assessment).
- **Progress & motivation** — watch swelling drop, pain scores fall, the day counter climb, and exercises get checked off.
- **Sign-in options:** Apple, Google, Email, or continue as guest.
- **Premium (optional):** 14-day free trial, then a monthly subscription. Premium adds advanced progress insights, full history depth, photo progress compare, and multiple simultaneous recoveries. The doctor report and its export are free forever and are never part of the premium offer.

**Positioning & tone:** Calm, credible, supportive, "physio-informed friend in your pocket." Reassuring without being clinical. The core emotional promise: **replace panic and guesswork with a clear plan and peace of mind.**

**Non-negotiable compliance (this app is NOT a medical device):**
- Never claim the app diagnoses, treats, cures, heals, or fixes anything, and never promise specific recovery timelines or guaranteed outcomes.
- Frame it as **educational guidance and a recovery companion** that complements — never replaces — professional care.
- Every angle must be compatible with a medical disclaimer and with encouraging users to seek professional/urgent care for severe or worsening symptoms.
- No fake testimonials, no fear-mongering, no "miracle cure" before/afters, and respect TikTok/Meta/X health-claim ad policies.

## User cases to script (cover each at least once)

1. **Fresh injury panic** — just rolled/sprained the ankle: "What do I even do right now?" → first-48-hours guidance + instant plan.
2. **Is it serious?** — "Sprained or broken? When should I actually worry?" → red-flag/Safety feature.
3. **Return to sport** — athlete afraid of coming back too early and re-injuring → return-to-sport readiness.
4. **Chronic instability** — an ankle that keeps rolling/giving way → strength + balance program.
5. **Post-surgery recovery** — unsure what movement is safe after surgery → guided phases.
6. **The appointment scene (my idea — write this one well):** Someone is stressed heading into a physio/doctor appointment and blanks on what to say or how to describe their progress. They open AnklePath, pull up their pain history, symptom log, tracker trends, and exportable report — and suddenly explain everything clearly and confidently. Emotional beat: *from flustered and forgettable → prepared and heard.*
7. **Physio is expensive / booked out** — a structured, affordable at-home companion **while** they wait or between sessions (complement, not replacement — keep it compliant).
8. **Random YouTube exercises** — "I was just doing random exercises hoping they'd help" → structured, safe, sourced-from-real-guidelines plan.
9. **Progress & motivation** — the satisfying arc: swelling down, pain score dropping, day counter climbing, exercises checked off.
10. **Stuck on the couch** — foot elevated, bored, doing nothing → "today's small steps" makes recovery feel active and finite.
