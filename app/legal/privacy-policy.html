Withdrawing consent

Open Profile → Health data consent to withdraw your health-data consent, or Profile → Product analytics to turn analytics off. Withdrawal takes effect immediately and does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand. Because the recovery plan cannot be personalised without health data, withdrawing consent stops that personalisation; to remove the data already stored, delete your account (below).

Service providers we use

We share limited information with trusted providers who process it on our behalf under data processing agreements:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — account sign-in and storage of your plan, logs, and onboarding answers.
  • PostHog — product analytics (only if you opt in).
  • Sentry — crash and error monitoring.
  • RevenueCat and the Apple App Store / Google Play — subscription entitlements and payment processing. Payment details are handled by the app stores; we never receive them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, and port your data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to withdraw consent at any time. In the app:

  • Access and portability: Profile → Export my data produces a machine-readable copy of everything we store about you.
  • Erasure: Profile → Delete account deletes your account and data immediately, from inside the app. You can also email us and we will complete verified requests within 30 days.
  • Rectification: You can edit your name and recovery inputs in the app.
  • Photo library: Choosing a profile photo is optional and only happens when you tap to add one.
  • If you are in California, you have comparable rights under the CCPA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

AnklePath is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All communication with our providers is encrypted in transit using TLS. Your data is stored in Google Firebase with access restricted to your own account, and access on our side is limited to what is needed to operate and support the app. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard your data.
